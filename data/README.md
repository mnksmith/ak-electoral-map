# Data

## Layout

- `raw/` — unmodified files downloaded from the Alaska Division of Elections and the Alaska GIS portal. Not committed; regenerated by `src/ak_electoral_map/fetch.py`.
- `processed/` — cleaned, joined precinct-level data produced by `src/ak_electoral_map/clean.py`. Committed (small).

## Provenance

Canonical sources and retrieval timestamps are tracked in `raw/MANIFEST.json`, written by the fetch script.

- 2024 general precinct results: https://www.elections.alaska.gov/results/24GENR/
- Precinct shapefile: https://gis.data.alaska.gov/ (Administrative Voting Precincts dataset)
